Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · applies to version 1.26.0 and later · Terms of use

In short

  • What you share with Screen2Act is analysed on your phone. It is never sent to us or to any cloud AI.
  • There is no Screen2Act account and no Screen2Act server. We cannot see your messages, files, calendar or contacts.
  • Google's on-phone components send Google basic diagnostics (such as phone model and app version), never your content.
  • You can erase everything the app has kept at any time.
  • Screen2Act is for adults (18+).

Who we are

Screen2Act™ is an Android app made by Demetrios Couppas, trading as Screen2Act, in the United Kingdom ("we"). He is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. To contact us about privacy or anything else, e-mail hello@screen2act.com.

What the app does with your content

You give Screen2Act content by sharing it from another app, choosing a file, pasting, typing or speaking. That can include messages, e-mails, screenshots, photos, PDFs, Word documents, calendar invites, contact cards and voice notes. The app reads it and suggests actions, such as a calendar entry, a reply, a call or directions.

  • Text in pictures and documents is read on your phone by Google ML Kit text recognition and barcode scanning.
  • Understanding and summarising is done on your phone by Gemini Nano, running in Android's AICore service. If Gemini Nano is not available, the app uses its own rules on the phone instead.
  • When you tap the microphone, your speech is turned into text by your phone's on-device speech recognition and is never sent online. The words appear in the text box for you to edit, like typed text, and no recording is kept. If your phone only offers online speech recognition, the in-app microphone isn't shown.
  • A voice note you share is turned into text on your phone by Google's ML Kit speech recognition, which uses the phone's on-device AI (Gemini Nano, where available) or a smaller on-device speech model. Google says these ML Kit features process the input and the results on the phone. If they can't be used, the app may try another speech service on the phone, but only one that says your language is installed for offline use, and it asks for offline recognition, so the recording isn't sent online. The recording is never sent to us.
  • Nothing is done until you approve it. Messages and e-mails are always drafts that open in your own apps for you to send.

What is stored, and where

Results, your inbox of saved actions and your settings (for example your name, sign-off and chosen calendar) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. By default, copies of the files you share are kept on the phone for 7 days, so inbox items can show where they came from, and are then deleted. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy ("Keep shared files for 7 days"), and shared files are then deleted once they've been read. Phone backups are switched off for this app, so this data is not copied to cloud backups.

Unsent drafts. If you type, paste, dictate or attach something but don't analyse it yet, the app keeps it in its private storage on your phone, so it isn't lost when you switch screens or Android closes the app. It stays until you analyse it or tap Start new. An attached file that is never analysed is deleted after the period set for keeping shared files (at least 1 day).

Voice notes that couldn't be read. If a shared voice note can't be turned into text in the sharing panel, a copy is kept in the app's private storage so you can try again in the app. It is deleted like other shared files.

The app keeps up to 200 past results. When a new result would go over that, the oldest is removed; the first time this happens, the app asks you first. Settings › Privacy › Clean up past results deletes results older than 1, 3 or 6 months, and you can delete a single day's or month's results under Recent. Clean-ups don't touch your inbox, reminders or calendar.

If you turn on the hidden Developer options (for testing), the app also keeps a log of its last 30 analyses on the phone, each with the first few words of what was shared, and the result of any speech test you run there. Clear log or Erase everything deletes it. A Developer option that is off unless you turn it on keeps the last shared voice note's converted audio, so it can be played back; only the latest is kept, and Erase everything deletes it. None of this leaves the phone unless you choose to open or share it in another app.

Settings › Privacy › Erase everything lists what it will delete, then deletes your inbox, past results and shared files, unsent drafts, scheduled reminders, and your details and settings straight away. It keeps only the date and version when the app was first installed, so that if paid features are added later, people who installed early keep what they had for free. Events you added to your calendar stay there, because they belong to your calendar app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything, including the install date.

When the app goes online

  • Reading a web page. Only when you share a link and tap "Read page", the app downloads that page the way a browser would. Nothing of yours is sent with the request.
  • Google components. Google ML Kit and AICore may download or update their on-phone models, including the speech recognition model the first time you share a voice note, and Android may download its UK English speech recognition model the first time you use the microphone. These are model downloads and never include your content or speech. ML Kit also sends Google diagnostics and usage data. That data covers device details (make, model, Android version), app details (package name and version), installation identifiers, performance metrics and error codes. It does not include the content you share. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking services of our own, and we do not sell or share your data.

Feedback you choose to send

The app never sends anything to us by itself. Two features prepare an e-mail in your own e-mail app, which you can read, edit or discard before sending:

  • Send feedback (Settings › About) adds your app version, phone model, Android version and whether Gemini Nano is on. If you leave "Include these counts" on, it also adds two numbers kept only on your phone: how many things you've shared and how many actions you've tapped.
  • Send this example to the developer (under a result) adds what you shared with Screen2Act for that result (the text, or for a screenshot, PDF or document, the text read from it; the file itself is not attached), the summary and actions the app suggested, and your app version, phone model, Android version and whether Gemini Nano or the app's built-in reading was used. What you shared may include other people's details, such as a sender's name or phone number, so check the e-mail and remove anything you don't want to send. We use those details only to fix the problem.

If you send one, we receive it as an ordinary e-mail at hello@screen2act.com. We use it only to answer you and to fix and improve Screen2Act. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in replying to you and improving the app. We keep e-mails, including any other people's details in them, for 12 months after our last contact with you, and then delete them. Ask us at any time to delete an e-mail you sent sooner.

Our mailbox is provided by 123 Reg (part of the GoDaddy group), and e-mails may also be kept in Google's Gmail. These providers store e-mails for us and may hold them outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they rely on the safeguards UK data protection law requires for international transfers.

Children

Screen2Act is for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. If you add your children's names and classes in Settings so replies to schools and clubs can be filled in, they stay on your phone like your other settings.
