What it does

Calendar

Dates go straight into the calendar you choose, including weekly classes, repeating meetings and term dates, with reminders for things to do.

Replies

Ready drafts for texts, WhatsApp and e-mail in your own sign-off, including “I'll be there” or “Can't make it” for invitations, and quote replies for work enquiries.

Contacts and calls

Save a new number from a message, or call the surgery, the garage or the customer in one tap.

Directions, links and codes

Open the map at the right address, join the meeting link and copy the booking reference or account number.

Who it's for

  • Anyone with a busy phone: appointments, deliveries, bookings and invitations from friends.
  • Families: school letters, clubs, parties and the group chat.
  • Work: meeting invites, e-mail threads and requests that need a reply.
  • Self-employed and small businesses: customer texts, quote requests and site visits, with your business details on replies.
  • Clubs and volunteers: newsletters, rotas and event dates, all added at once.

Private by design

What you share is read on your phone by its built-in AI (Gemini Nano). On phones without it, Screen2Act uses its own on-phone reading. There's no Screen2Act account, no server and no cloud AI, and nothing happens until you approve it.
